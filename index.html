How to Use the Command Center
Your daily operating system. Here's exactly where each tab fits in your day.
My Desk — Your Anchor
When: First thing every morning. Glance before your first call.
What it shows: Active searches and candidate status, top BD leads, hot candidates, key rules.
How to update: At EOD, paste Claude's full EOD response into the Update box and click Update. My Desk rebuilds automatically from Claude's text — no manual entry needed.
Download for Tomorrow: Hit this at EOD. Paste that file into Claude tomorrow morning along with Business Truth and System Truth.
Copy for Claude: Copies your desk state mid-day if you need it in a new session.
Daily Plan — Your Day Structure
When: Fill it out each morning after your Claude briefing. Check goals off as you complete them.
Five buckets: Recruiting, Delivery, BD, Admin, Personal. At least one goal per bucket.
Carry system: When you close the day, incomplete goals appear in a confirm dialog. Click OK to carry them to tomorrow. They show up the next morning marked "Carry."
Share Plan with Claude: Copies your plan to paste into Claude to lock in your day.
Close Out Day (in nav bar): Archives today and carries selected goals forward.
Daily Capture — Your Live Log
When: After every call, email, text, or LI message — log it immediately. Fast and clumsy. Don't clean it up.
New — Location/Market field: Always capture what market you were working. This feeds your intelligence layer over time.
New — File Intel As: When logging a BD/client call, pick a category to auto-file the intel directly into the Intelligence tab. No extra step.
End of Day Review: Click this at EOD. Builds your full log summary to paste into Claude.
Rule: If it's not logged, it doesn't exist.
Intelligence — Your Market Brain
When: Add intel during or after calls. Search it before BD calls to know what to lead with.
Six categories: Company Signals, Talent Movement, Market Signals, Comp Data, Relationship Paths, Timing Flags.
Two ways to add: Click + Add Intel manually, or use "File Intel As" in Daily Capture while logging a call — it files automatically.
Search: Type any keyword. Pulls across all six categories instantly.
Market Brief: One click builds a shareable market snapshot for BD calls. This is what separates you from a resume slinger.
Week Plan — Your Weekly Compass
When: Monday morning. Set your week before you start calling.
Fill in: Active searches and priorities, hot candidates to market and who to call, your top 5 goals, top 5 markets, and weekly metric targets.
Build Weekly Summary for Claude: Use on Fridays to get your weekly wrap and carry forward.
Close Out Week: Archives the week on Fridays.
The Daily Loop — Start Here
Morning (15 min): My Desk → Download for Tomorrow → Open Claude → Paste Business Truth + System Truth + My Desk file → Paste overnight messages → Get briefing → Fill Daily Plan → Share Plan with Claude → Go to Daily Capture → Work.

During the day: Log every interaction in Daily Capture immediately after it happens. Use "File Intel As" when you hear something worth keeping.

EOD (15 min): Daily Capture → End of Day Review → Copy + Open Claude → Paste → Get seven things → Paste CRM notes → Update My Desk → Download for Tomorrow → Close Out Day → Done.
